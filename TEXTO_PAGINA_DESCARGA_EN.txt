HOW TO INSTALL AUTODIRECTOR PRO
===============================

1. Download the installer (Instalador-AutoDirectorPro.exe) and open it.

2. If Windows shows a blue window saying "Windows protected your PC":
   this is normal. It appears for programs from independent creators that
   don't have a digital signature yet, which is why Windows says "Unknown publisher".
     - Click "More info".
     - Click "Run anyway".
   This only happens the first time.

3. Follow the installer steps (you can choose English or Spanish at the start).
   It does not need administrator permissions.

4. Open AutoDirector Pro from the Start menu or the desktop.

5. Language: top right of the program, choose "English" or "Español" at any
   time. The program remembers your choice.


IF YOUR BROWSER OR ANTIVIRUS BLOCKS IT
- Chrome or Edge may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Click the three
  dots on the download warning and choose "Keep" (or "Keep anyway").
- Some antivirus programs wrongly flag programs like this one. Allow the file
  or add it to your antivirus exceptions.
